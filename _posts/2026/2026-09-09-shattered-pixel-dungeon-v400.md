## What's new in v4.0.0

v4.0.0 is arguably Shattered's biggest update yet! There's a massive new quest, a bunch of new in-game art, 6 new enchantments, and plenty smaller changes and adjustments.

I talked about this update in four previous blog posts:
- [Coming Soon to Shattered: The Vault Quest!](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/coming-soon-to-shattered-the-vault-quest.html)
- [Status Update: v4.0 Beta Soon!](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/status-update-v40-beta-soon.html)
- [Coming Soon to Shattered: New Enchants and Pixel Art!](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/coming-soon-to-shattered-new-enchants-and-pixel-art.html)
- [Coming Soon to Shattered: A New City Quest!](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/coming-soon-to-shattered-a-new-city-quest.html)

Here's the full changelog for v4.0.0! Click or tap on each entry to read more. Note that this changelog will become slightly outdated as patches are released and dev commentary is added, so check the changes screen in-game for the most up to date version.

**New Content**

<ul>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Dev Commentary</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
- Released September 9th, 2026
- 281 days after v3.3.0 (175 days after v3.3.8)

Dev commentary will be added here in the future.
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>New City Quest!</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
**The Ambitious Imp's quest has been totally redone, into easily the largest quest yet!**

The Imp now tasks you with raiding an ancient dwarven vault, filled with hazards and loot! Be careful though, as you won't be able to take your gear with you! The quest includes:

- A massive new sub-region roughly the size of three dungeon floors, featuring over 20 new room types!
- Three new static hazards that are integrated into ~10 of the new rooms!
- New variants of enemies ranging from the prisons to the city!
- An entire second progression experience, with easy low tier loot and well-defended high tier loot!
- A new boss that's specially designed to test the skills of vault plunderers!

Of course, adventurers need a suitable reward ;). If you're successful in the vault, the Imp will let you take **a single item out with you!** Many items in the vault are at a similar power level to his classic reward of a highly upgraded ring, but you'll get a much bigger variety of choice. He'll also continue to set up shop later as usual.
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>New Environment Visuals</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
**After almost two years since I initially teased it, the first wave of visual improvements to Shattered's in-game pixel art are finally here!**

Lots of dungeon environments have been improved or given new details by **Aleksandar Komitov**! This time around there's one important universal change and then a focus on the city region and quest rooms:

- There is now shadowing where walls meet floor.
- The City now has a new kind of special floor tile.
- Carpets are now a custom visual that can be applied on top of regular tiles anywhere! They are currently only used in the dwarven city.
- Enhanced visuals at the city entrance and exit.
- Overhauled visuals for the caves quest & blacksmith.
- Overhauled Mass Grave and Ritual rooms from the prison quest.
- Improvements to sewer barrels, barricades, and city flaming pedestals.

I expect to continue releasing waves of new art and tweaks each major update for the forseeable future, **please let me know what you think!**
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>New Consumable Item Sprites!</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
**After almost two years since I initially teased it, the first wave of visual improvements to Shattered's in-game pixel art are finally here!**

Most of Shattered's consumable items have been given overhauled sprites by **PumpkinVolt**! This time around we focused on consumable items, and some items directly related to those consumables. The following sprites have been redone:

- Seeds, darts, potions, & elixirs/brews
- Runestones, scrolls, & spell items
- Dewdrops & petrified seed (related to seeds)
- Liquid metal & blood vial (related to potions)
- Energy crystals & exotic crystals (related to spells)
- Dwarf Tokens & other new quest items

I expect to continue releasing waves of new art and tweaks each major update for the forseeable future, **please let me know what you think!**
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>New Enchantments!</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
**Four new enchantments and two new curses** have been added for weapons! Long-time players will recognize several of these as reworked enchantments that were previously removed years ago.

- **Venomous** is a returning common enchant that can apply stacking poison on a delay.
- **Vorpal** is a returning uncommon enchant that can convert damage into non-stacking bleed.
- **Eldritch** is a returning uncommon enchant that can apply fear to all nearby enemies except your primary target.
- **Crystal** is a new rare enchant that boosts damage, but requires you to work around limited durability.
- **Pressurized** is a new curse that can create a geyser of water, pushing both you and enemies away.
- **Wondrous** is a new curse that can trigger random cursed wand effects.
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Swarm Intelligence Overhaul</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
The swarm intelligence challenge is getting adjustments to improve counterplay and consistency.

Prior to v4.0 swarm triggered with a static 8 tiles of range only when an enemy initially noticed you. Experienced players found a bunch of ways to avoid triggering the effect entirely though.

The challenge is being adjusted to now trigger constantly whenever an enemy sees you, but with a range of only 2 tiles that increases up to 12 over time. Breaking sight in any way, even for a moment, resets this range. This way tricksy gameplay is still rewarded, but in a way that's more visible and easy to access.

A visual buff has also been added that shows you the current alert range.
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>New Enemy AI</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
As part of the new vault quest, I've added a separate style of enemy behaviour that's meant to work with stealth gameplay! Enemies using this AI mode have these differences:

- Nearby enemy movement can be seen through walls.
- **Sleeping** enemies have reduced detection range.
- **Wandering** enemies patrol pre-set routes and have a sharply reduced detection range behind themselves if they are moving.
- **Investigating** is a new AI state that exists between wandering/sleeping and attacking. Investigating enemies will move toward you, but don't attack until they detect you again. Investigating enemies can lose sight of the player easily, so ducking behind doors or corners is very effective.
- If an enemy is attacked, they skip investigating and immediately start retaliating, just like normal.

Currently this new AI mode is only used in the new vault quest, all other enemies in the dungeon should be unchanged.
</blockquote></details></li>
</ul>

**Changes**

<ul>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Existing Enchant Changes</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
With new enchantments being added, I've also taken the oppourtunity to improve on some quirks relating to existing enchants:

- **Kinetic** conserved damage is no longer lost (but still isn't increased) if the hit from kinetic is cancelled due to the enemy first dieing to another on-hit effect (e.g. smite)
- **Corrupting** can now apply if ANY part of the corrupting hit kills (e.g. smite), not just the hit from the corrupting weapon itself.
- **Grim** now triggers more consistently if extra damage (e.g. smite, holy weapon) applies before the hit from the grim weapon itself.
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Change Screen Adjustments</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
With another major release coming to Shattered, I've decided to look back and make a few adjustments to spruce up the changes screen.

- Changes screen is now split into post-release (v1.0+) and pre-release (v0.9.3-), this gives enough space for tabs for each major version (with v0.5 and older still grouped together)
- Change screen icons now use historical visuals that are accurate to the update being covered. I felt this change was especially important now that we're starting a big in-game art expansion.
- Added change history for the original Pixel Dungeon at the end of the list. This includes original comments made by Watabou for each update!
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>10+ Misc. Changes</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
**Highlights:**
- Health bars now visually darken to show the total amount of incoming damage over time
- Targeted cell VFX for incoming enemy attacks now persist until the attack occurs
- Added better support for 3rd party Android appstores that handle updates themselves
- Various internal code improvements to music playback, should reduce stuttering/glitching
- Defeating the caves quest boss now shows a 'boss beaten' slain
- Made UI/UX improvements to stone of augmentation
- Lowered the ( and ) symbols in pixel font for better consistency with regular fonts. Makes for better text emotes too ;)

**Other:**
- Updated credits based on new artist contributions
- Alchemy and well water VFX are now visible in the fog of war
- Items are now pushed out of doors that become locked by bosses
- Dropped support for savegames prior to v3.1.1 
- Performance improvements to blue 'checked cell' visuals, red targeted cell indicators, and skeleton key spectral walls.
- Updated various code dependencies
</blockquote></details></li>

- Blood vial causing healing effects to stack when they shouldn't
- Mossy clump changing level shape for runs on the same seed

**Effects:**

**Items:**

- VFX from flow and swiftness glyph being visible in the fog of war

- Wild magic zapping with more wands than intended in some cases

- Fireblast applying paralysis to downed ghouls

**Enemies:**

- Various cases where chasms could cause enemy deaths to trigger twice

**Buffs**

<ul>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Item Buffs</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
While v4.0 is mainly focused on new content, there are a few balance changes I'm making as well. Several items are getting some boosts:

- **Force Cube** base damage up to 10-30 from 10-25

- **Footwear of Nature** charge speed scaling with level increased, up to +50% at +10
- **Dried Rose** ghost HP up to 40+10*lvl, from 20+8*lvl, and ghost damage now scales with strength, just like the hero
- **Master Thieves' Armband** charge speed now scales with level, up to +50% at +10

- **Exotic Crystals** conversion chance up to 20% per level, from 12.5%
- **Chaotic Censer** is now much more likely to spew gasses that actually harm the enemy
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Talent/Ability Buffs</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
A couple of abilities/talents are also getting buffs:

- **Combo Strike** Duelist ability now grants 15 turns of duration on-kill, just like Gladiator's combo
- **Feint** armor ability base cost decreased to 35 from 50 (cost swapped with challenge)
</blockquote></details></li>
</ul>

![](/assets/images/{{page.date|date:'%Y/%Y-%m-%d'}}/nerfs.png){: .align-center}
**Nerfs**

<ul>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Item Nerfs</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
A few items were also showing as quite strong and are getting scaled back a bit.

Most notably, I'm returning runestones of aggression back to their pre-v1.0 behaviour vs. bosses. In retrospect, while I do want them to be usable to redirect aggression during boss fights, letting them apply directly to bosses was a mistake. You can still use aggro stones on boss minions to take aggression off yourself during boss fights, and everthing ganging up on one minion is a more suitable scaled-back version of the scroll of rage effect.

- **Stone of Aggression** can no longer be directly applied to bosses
- **Ring of Haste** speed boost per level down to +15%, from +17.5%
- **Spyglass** downside made more signiciant, bonus item opacity down to 10% from 15%
</blockquote></details></li>
<li><details markdown="1"><summary>Talent/Ability Nerfs</summary><blockquote markdown="1">
A few targeted nerfs/adjustments to hero abilities or talents. Some of these are general and some are aimed at preventing cheese in the early stages of the new quest:

These changes are general (although barkskin was also far too strong in the vault):
- **Barkskin** talent no longer triggers from furrowed grass, but does now trigger from plants
- **Barkskin** talent amount per level reduced to 33% of hero level, from 50%
- **Challenge** armor ability base cost increased to 50 from 35 (cost swapped with feint)

These adjustments are aimed at the new vault area specifically:
- **Hold Fast** talent now requires the Warrior's seal, or has a cap based on worn armor if used by other heroes
- **Monk** no longer retains energy over revives or entry into the vault
</blockquote></details></li>
</ul>
